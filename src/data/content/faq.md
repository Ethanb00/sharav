items:
  - q: "How hot is the zhug, really?"
    a: "Hot. It's a condiment, not a dip - a spoonful stirred into hummus or spread thin on bread. Everything else in the lineup is mild."
  - q: "How long does it keep?"
    a: "Every container is marked with a best-by date. Nothing is preserved or pasteurized, so keep it cold and use your best judgment."
  - q: "Allergens?"
    a: "Sesame in everything with tahini. Walnuts in the muhammara, pistachios in the carrot dip. All made in one kitchen, so we can't promise no cross-contact."
  - q: "Is it vegan? Kosher?"
    a: "The dips are all plant-based. We're not certified kosher - ask us and we'll tell you exactly how we cook."
