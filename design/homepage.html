Our Story

Foods & Flavors of the Desert

Mizrahi cuisine is the food of the ancient Jewish communities from the Middle East, Levant, and North Africa - Iraq, Iran, Yemen, Morocco, Tunisia, Libya, Syria, and Egypt - blending Levantine, Persian, and North African traditions.

A blend of cuisines developed over generations, formed by culture, time, and the flavors of the desert and the trade routes of the Silk Road.

“To bring the warmth, depth, and story of Mizrahi cuisine to our community.”

On the Menu

Made in small batches, market to market

Using the finest ingredients, we produce a selection of fresh spreads, pita, and condiments. We also have free mint tea for you to enjoy, so stop by!

Pita

Fresh-baked and served warm, our pita puffs in a blazing oven and comes out soft, pillowy, and made for scooping. Nothing like the grocery store version.

Hummus

Slow-simmered chickpeas blended with tahini, lemon, and garlic until impossibly smooth, finished with a pour of olive oil, pine nuts, and sumac.

Zhug

The fiery Yemenite green condiment: cilantro, parsley, hot chilies, and garlic ground with toasted cardamom, cumin, and coriander. Bright, herbal, and hot. A little goes a long way.

Baba Ganoush

Whole eggplants charred over open flame until they collapse, then folded with tahini and lemon. Deeply smoky, silky, and a little rustic.

Muhammara

A Syrian spread of roasted red peppers and walnuts with pomegranate molasses and Aleppo pepper. Sweet, tart, nutty, and gently spiced.

Roasted Za'atar Carrot Dip

Our signature dish. Carrots roasted with garlic and za'atar until sweet and caramelized, blended with tahini and pistachios, and topped with chopped pistachios and Aleppo pepper. Earthy, warm, rich, and satisfying.
