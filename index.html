Our Story

Distinct. Authentic. Underrepresented.

Mizrahi cuisine is the food of Jewish communities from the Middle East and North Africa — Iraq, Iran, Yemen, Morocco, Tunisia, Libya, Syria, and Egypt — blending Levantine, Persian, and North African traditions. Think bold spices, slow-cooked meats, pickled vegetables, flaky flatbreads, and condiments like amba (pickled mango) and zhug (green chili sauce). It's not generic "Mediterranean" food — it's a cuisine with deep cultural roots and a story rarely told outside the community that keeps it alive.

"To bring the warmth, depth, and story of Mizrahi Jewish cuisine to our community — one plate at a time."

On the Menu

Made in small batches, market to market

Our lineup is a rotating, farmers-market-friendly selection from a much bigger family of recipes. Ask at the booth what's fresh this week.

Sabich

Iraqi-Israeli pita stuffed with fried eggplant, egg, hummus, amba, and tahini.

Malawach

Yemenite flaky layered flatbread, served with crushed tomato and zhug.

Burekas

Flaky phyllo pastries filled with potato, spinach-feta, or mushroom.

Shakshuka

North African eggs poached low and slow in a spiced tomato-pepper sauce.

Jachnun

Slow-rolled, caramelized Yemenite pastry — a Shabbat morning tradition.

Mezze Plate

Matbucha, baba ganoush, and harissa carrot salad, with warm pita on the side.
